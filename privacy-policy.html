Welcome to Knowlina

Welcome to Knowlina (“Knowlina,” “we,” “our,” or “us”). Knowlina is an educational and parental supervision platform designed for children and families.

We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy, safety, and security of all our users—especially children. This Privacy Policy outlines how Knowlina accesses, collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use our mobile application and related services.

01

Child Safety and Parental Consent

Child safety and compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policies are foundational to Knowlina:

  • Parental Authorization: Children cannot independently register for an account. All child profiles must be created, authorized, and linked to a verified parent or legal guardian account.
  • Affirmative Consent: By setting up a child profile, parents grant explicit consent for educational progress tracking and parental safety features.
  • No Social Profiling: Children have no public profile and cannot interact with unknown external parties.
  • Zero Advertising: We never serve targeted commercial or behavioral ads to children.
  • Parental Rights: Parents may review, modify, or permanently delete child data at any time.
02

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide educational and safety services:

  • Account Data: Parent name, email address, phone number, and encrypted credentials.
  • Child Profile: Child name/nickname, avatar image, age, and assigned grade/educational levels.
  • Educational Progress: Completed learning missions, quizzes, stars, coins, and achievement badges.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and anonymous performance logs.
  • Location Data: Geographic GPS coordinates collected for parental tracking.
03

Background Location Access

Knowlina accesses the ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION permission on child devices under the following strict conditions:

  • What is collected: Precise device GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, timestamp).
  • When it is collected: Even when the app is closed or not in active use, provided the user has granted permission.
  • Why it is collected: To allow parents and legal guardians to see their child’s live location on the Parent Dashboard, ensuring child safety and peace of mind when travelling to school or outdoors.
  • Prominent Disclosure: Before requesting system permission, a dedicated full-screen disclosure is presented explaining why background location is needed. Permission is requested only after the user taps 'Agree'.
  • Sharing: Location data is encrypted and strictly shared only with the linked parent account. It is NEVER sold, shared with third-party advertisers, or used for commercial tracking.
04

Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell personal data. We partner only with trusted service providers to run app infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase: Infrastructure and push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
  • Google Sign-In: Optional secure OAuth authentication for parents.
  • RevenueCat: Mobile subscription and in-app purchase validation.
  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchase and subscription transactions on Android.
  • Airwallex: Secure payment gateway processing for subscription tiers.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim: Geocoding street names for parent tracking.
05

Data Security

All data transmissions between the Knowlina app and our backend servers are secured using TLS/HTTPS encryption. Sensitive data is stored in secure, access-controlled databases protected by modern firewalls and authentication safeguards.

06

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal data only for as long as your account remains active.

  • In-App Deletion: Parents can permanently delete their account and all associated child data at any time inside the app by going to Profile > Account > Delete Account.
  • Immediate Purge: Account deletion permanently and irrevocably removes all parent credentials, child profiles, and location logs from our active production systems.
  • Email Requests: You can also request complete data deletion by emailing privacy@knowlina.com.
07

Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to access, export, rectify, or delete your family's personal data.
